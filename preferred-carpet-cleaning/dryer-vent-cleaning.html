Signs

When it’s time

  • Clothes need more than one cycle to dry
  • The laundry room feels hot or humid
  • A burning smell when the dryer runs
  • The vent outside barely moves air
Process

How it works

  1. Check

    We look at the vent run and where it exits.

  2. Clean

    We clean the vent path with the right approach for the setup, then check that air is moving properly.

  3. Test

    We confirm air flows freely before we leave.
