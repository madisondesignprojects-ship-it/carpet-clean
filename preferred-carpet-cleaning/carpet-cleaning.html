Carpet cleaning

What the service does

What are you actually paying to have cleaned?

Carpet holds onto more than the loose dirt you can see. Foot traffic works soil into the pile, spills can leave spots behind, and repeated DIY cleaning can leave moisture or product residue that affects how the carpet looks and feels.

Loose and embedded soil

Professional cleaning is designed to reach beyond routine vacuuming and remove soil that has worked down into the carpet fibers.

Spots and staining

Problem areas are looked at individually because stain type, age, fiber and previous treatment can all change the result.

Traffic lanes

High-traffic areas often collect more soil than surrounding carpet. The cleaning approach can be adjusted to those areas.

Stairs and edges

Stairs, hallways, entry areas and edges can receive the same attention as open rooms, depending on the scope of the job.

The cleaning process

A carpet cleaning appointment, step by step.

A good cleaning starts with understanding the carpet instead of treating every room exactly the same.

  1. Inspect

    We look over the areas being cleaned, note traffic, spots, odors and carpet condition, and talk through anything that needs special attention.

  2. Prepare

    Loose soil and problem areas are addressed before the main cleaning begins so the deeper cleaning step can do its job more effectively.

  3. Deep clean

    The carpet is cleaned and the loosened soil and cleaning solution are extracted using an approach suited to the material and condition.

  4. Finish

    We review the result with you and explain what to expect while the carpet dries and how to care for it afterward.

Results vary by carpet

Why one stain comes out and another may not.

Professional cleaning can make a major difference, but it cannot guarantee that every mark disappears. The material itself, the stain, how long it has been there and what has already been used on it all matter.

  • Carpet fiber and construction
  • Age and type of the stain
  • Previous spot-cleaning products or treatments
  • Wear, discoloration or permanent fiber damage
  • How heavily the area is used
Before we arrive

A little preparation goes a long way.

The exact preparation depends on the home and the scope of the job, but clearing fragile items and making the carpeted areas accessible gives the technician room to work.

Helpful things to do beforehand

  • Pick up small or fragile items from the areas being cleaned.
  • Make sure the technician can reach the carpeted areas you requested.
  • Keep pets safely away from the work area while cleaning is underway.
  • Point out stains, odors or areas you especially want addressed.
After the cleaning

The carpet needs time to finish drying.

Freshly cleaned carpet can still feel damp when the technician leaves. Drying time depends on the cleaning method, carpet construction, moisture level, airflow and conditions in the room. We’ll explain what to expect before we go.
